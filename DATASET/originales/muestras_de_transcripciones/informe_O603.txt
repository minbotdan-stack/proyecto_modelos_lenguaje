Sample Name: General Medicine Followup - Hypertension and Bowel Symptoms

Medical Specialty:

Consult - History and Phy.

Description: Follow-up for blood pressure, intermittent knee pain, and urgency with occasional stool leakage.

(Medical Transcription Sample Report)

INTERVAL HISTORY: The patient reports weight loss and improvement in blood pressure after a medication dose increase. He stopped a newly prescribed cholesterol medication because he felt poorly while taking it. Intermittent knee pain persists, for which he has used naproxen in occasional excessive doses. He also reports straining with bowel movements, frequent stools with urgency and several episodes of staining or inability to reach the toilet. Symptoms have been present for years and are embarrassing. He has a history of cholecystectomy. Alcohol consumption has been reduced after noticing impaired performance while officiating sporting events.

MEDICATIONS: Antihypertensive medication and intermittent naproxen. Other medications reviewed for refill.

REVIEW OF SYSTEMS: No significant chest pain or shortness of breath reported. Mixed stool consistency without persistent frank diarrhea.

ASSESSMENT AND PLAN:
1. Hypertension: Improved on the current increased dose; continue monitoring.
2. Hyperlipidemia: Patient discontinued prescribed lipid therapy after adverse symptoms; recent weight loss and lifestyle measures reviewed.
3. Knee pain: Naproxen may be taken when needed but should not exceed twice-daily dosing. Discussed gastrointestinal and renal risks of NSAID overuse and use of alternative analgesia when appropriate.
4. Urgency, straining, and occasional fecal leakage: Trial of higher dietary fiber, fiber supplementation, and stool softener. Avoid straining. Consider Gastroenterology referral if symptoms persist.
5. Medication renewals: Prescriptions reviewed, including a limited-use medication requiring periodic renewal.

Return in approximately six months or sooner for persistent bowel symptoms.
