Sample Name: Routine Preventive Followup

Medical Specialty:

Consult - History and Phy.

Description: Short routine visit reviewing blood pressure, preventive care, skin treatment, and prior musculoskeletal complaints.

(Medical Transcription Sample Report)

HISTORY OF PRESENT ILLNESS: The patient attends earlier than his intended six-month to one-year follow-up interval and reports feeling generally well. He has ongoing treatment for skin lesions with topical cream and expects reassessment with possible freezing of remaining lesions. A previous Achilles complaint has not significantly progressed. Blood pressure has improved, with systolic reading approximately 125 today compared with a previous value around 130. Immunization status was reviewed, including prior Tdap. Colonoscopy and prostate evaluation have been discussed previously, as has diabetes screening. He reports no new active medical complaints.

IMPRESSION AND PLAN: Routine follow-up with improved blood pressure and no new acute issues. Continue previously arranged dermatologic management and preventive screening at recommended intervals. Prior immunization records were reviewed. No additional treatment changes were required; return in the late summer or early fall, or sooner if new symptoms occur.
