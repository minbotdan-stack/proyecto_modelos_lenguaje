Sample Name: General Medicine Visit - Knee Pain and Sexual Dysfunction

Medical Specialty:

Consult - History and Phy.

Description: Persistent knee pain and medication-related erectile dysfunction in a patient receiving psychiatric therapy.

(Medical Transcription Sample Report)

HISTORY OF PRESENT ILLNESS: The patient reports continuing knee pain and tenderness, exacerbated by prolonged walking, with occasional catching or locking. Recent MRI and CT imaging were reviewed by specialists, and surgery or knee replacement was deferred. Naproxen has provided little relief. He also requests Podiatry assessment for recurrent calluses and treatment for erectile dysfunction, which he associates with psychiatric medications used for bipolar illness. Similar symptoms previously improved when medication was stopped, but he wishes to maintain treatment rather than discontinue it. He reports approximately six months without cocaine or opiate use. He continues smoking, though less than previously.

PAST MEDICAL HISTORY: Bipolar disorder, chronic knee pain, foot calluses and prior substance use.

ASSESSMENT AND PLAN:
1. Chronic knee pain: Reviewed recent imaging; additional radiography not required presently. Trial nonopioid analgesic therapy, provide supportive knee sleeve, and refer to Physical Therapy.
2. Erectile dysfunction, possibly medication-related: Trial of vardenafil discussed, including the restricted quantity available through the pharmacy. Continue psychiatric treatment and follow response.
3. Recurrent plantar callus: Refer to Podiatry.
4. Substance use disorder: Congratulated patient on abstinence and continued recovery. Avoid opioid analgesics given substance use history.
5. Tobacco use: Discussed behavioral smoking-reduction strategies and secondhand exposure.
6. Preventive care: Order lipid and chemistry testing, review immunizations and follow-up of colorectal screening.

Patient directed to laboratory and prosthetics service for the brace as arranged.
