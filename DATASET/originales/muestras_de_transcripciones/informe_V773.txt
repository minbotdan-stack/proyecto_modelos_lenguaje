Sample Name: General Medicine Followup - Hypertension and Prediabetes

Medical Specialty:

Consult - History and Phy.

Description: Difficult-to-control blood pressure with elevated glucose and early renal risk markers.

(Medical Transcription Sample Report)

HISTORY OF PRESENT ILLNESS: The patient returns with persistent mildly elevated blood pressure despite taking several antihypertensive agents. He monitors pressure at home and is frustrated by repeated adjustments. Office systolic pressure decreases from approximately 156 to 148 with repeat measurement. Laboratory work has shown elevated glucose consistent with prediabetes rather than established diabetes and a small amount of urinary protein with concern for early renal effects of hypertension. He is overweight and wishes to manage his condition with diet and walking. He also has recurrent gout symptoms and questions the number of medications required.

PAST MEDICAL HISTORY: Hypertension, impaired glucose regulation, obesity and gout.

MEDICATIONS: Multiple antihypertensives including amlodipine, lisinopril and a thiazide diuretic; gout treatment reviewed.

ASSESSMENT AND PLAN:
1. Hypertension: After considering lisinopril escalation, the final plan was to increase the thiazide from a half to a whole tablet daily because the pharmacy would not authorize the proposed lisinopril change. Continue home blood pressure log and return for nurse review in one month. Add or modify therapy if still above goal.
2. Prediabetes and obesity: Extensive counseling concerning reduced sugars, healthier fats, vegetables, fish, regular walking and weight loss. No diabetes drug initiated.
3. Early renal risk/proteinuria: Emphasized blood pressure control and ongoing renal laboratory assessment.
4. Gout: Discussed restarting preventive medication and obtaining uric acid testing; distinguished prophylaxis from acute flare treatment.
5. Preventive care: Colonoscopy and influenza vaccination discussed, medications renewed.

Routine physician follow-up in one year with interim blood pressure assessment.
