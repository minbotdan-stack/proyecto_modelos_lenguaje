Sample Name: Breast Cancer Oncology Consultation Chemotherapy Note

Medical Specialty:

Consult - History and Phy.

Description: Oncology consultation for breast cancer diagnosis, chemotherapy treatment, hormone therapy, lumpectomy, radiation oncology referral.

(Medical Transcription Sample Report)

CHIEF COMPLAINT: New diagnosis of breast cancer and discussion of cancer treatment options.

HISTORY OF PRESENT ILLNESS: The patient is a 49-year-old female referred to the oncology clinic after an abnormal screening mammogram and core needle biopsy confirming invasive ductal carcinoma of the left breast. She reports a two-month history of a painless breast lump without skin changes or nipple discharge. There is significant anxiety regarding prognosis, chemotherapy side effects, and long-term survival. She is interested in understanding advanced breast cancer treatment options, including targeted therapy and hormone therapy.

PAST MEDICAL HISTORY: Hypertension, hypothyroidism, and obesity. No prior history of malignancy. Family history is positive for breast cancer in her mother and maternal aunt.

MEDICATIONS: Lisinopril 20 mg daily, Levothyroxine 88 mcg daily, multivitamin.

ALLERGIES: No known drug allergies.

PHYSICAL EXAMINATION: Vital signs stable. Examination of the left breast reveals a 2.5 cm firm, non-tender mass in the upper outer quadrant, no skin dimpling, and no nipple retraction. Palpation of the axilla reveals a small, mobile lymph node. No hepatosplenomegaly or bony tenderness.

INVESTIGATIONS: Mammogram and ultrasound demonstrate a suspicious breast lesion, BI-RADS 5. Core biopsy confirms grade II invasive ductal carcinoma, ER/PR positive, HER2 negative. Staging CT of chest, abdomen, and pelvis shows no evidence of distant metastasis. Baseline labs and liver function tests are within normal limits.

ASSESSMENT: Stage II left breast cancer, hormone receptor positive, HER2 negative, with involvement of a single axillary lymph node.

PLAN:
1. Discussed comprehensive breast cancer treatment including lumpectomy with sentinel lymph node biopsy followed by adjuvant chemotherapy and radiation therapy.
2. Recommend adjuvant chemotherapy regimen with dose-dense doxorubicin and cyclophosphamide followed by paclitaxel, with growth factor support as needed.
3. Following chemotherapy and radiation, initiate hormone therapy with Tamoxifen for at least 5 years.

4. Referred to radiation oncology for evaluation of local control options and to a breast surgeon experienced in advanced breast cancer surgery.
5. Provided counseling on fertility preservation, genetic testing for BRCA mutation, and supportive care resources including pain management and nutritional counseling during cancer treatment.
