TITLE: Followup for Mild Hypercalcemia
DESCRIPTION: Routine medical followup with laboratory findings suggesting hyperparathyroidism.

HISTORY OF PRESENT ILLNESS: The patient presents for a routine appointment. He reports no new major complaints. A recent dermatologic lesion was removed without reported concern for malignancy. Blood pressure is controlled on his existing medication, and previous cholesterol testing was satisfactory. He continues to follow with cardiology and urology. Prior screening revealed a mildly elevated serum calcium level. Subsequent blood testing suggested hyperparathyroidism. The nature of the parathyroid glands and the significance of their possible overactivity were explained. He also requests renewal of a previously prescribed topical treatment for dry feet.

PHYSICAL EXAMINATION: Blood pressure is satisfactory. The heart and lungs were examined. No significant peripheral edema was described.

ASSESSMENT AND PLAN:
1. Mild hypercalcemia with biochemical concern for hyperparathyroidism. Endocrinology consultation was ordered. Additional blood and urine testing is requested before the specialty visit.
2. Hypertension, adequately controlled. Continue existing medications and obtain refills as needed.
3. Dry skin of the feet. Prior topical treatment was reviewed and medication requests addressed.
4. Ongoing cardiovascular and prostate followup. Continue established specialty appointments.

The patient was instructed to obtain laboratory specimens and arrange his endocrinology visit. Routine followup in approximately six months.
