TITLE: General Medicine Followup - Cardiac and Pulmonary History
DESCRIPTION: Routine medication follow-up in a patient receiving cardiac rehabilitation and asthma therapy.

INTERVAL HISTORY: The patient reports doing generally well, though arthritis continues to cause pain. He recently underwent repeat operations for carpal tunnel syndrome in both hands. A cardiac stress study earlier in the year was negative. He also underwent a sleep study because of concern for sleep apnea and nighttime slow heart rates, with specialist interpretation pending. He reports longstanding intermittent cardiac rhythm irregularity, improved symptoms while taking magnesium, and regular cardiac rehabilitation exercise three times per week. He is adherent to his medications but has difficulty obtaining montelukast in a 90-day supply. He reports intermittent albuterol use and questions his prior combination inhaler treatment.

PAST MEDICAL HISTORY: Cardiac rhythm symptoms, asthma, arthritis, recurrent carpal tunnel syndrome, and possible sleep-disordered breathing under evaluation.

PHYSICAL EXAMINATION: Blood pressure satisfactory. No peripheral edema reported; heart and lungs examined.

ASSESSMENT AND PLAN:
1. Cardiac history: Stable clinically with negative recent stress testing and ongoing outside Cardiology care. Continue rehabilitation.
2. Possible sleep apnea: Follow up with sleep specialist for results and management.
3. Asthma: Reconcile inhaler use and montelukast supply; continue appropriate therapy.
4. Arthritis and prior hand surgery: Continue current management.
5. Hyperlipidemia: Review pending lipid panel and communicate results by letter.
6. Medication access: Reviewed renewals and dispensing limitations.

Patient may return in six months or sooner for medication or clinical concerns; also receives routine outside primary care.
