TITLE: Medical Followup - Coronary Stent and Medication Nonadherence
DESCRIPTION: Hypertension and cardiac medication reconciliation after myocardial infarction and stent placement.

HISTORY OF PRESENT ILLNESS: The patient suffered a myocardial infarction with stent placement in August. He subsequently stopped most of his cardiovascular medications because of severe headaches beginning after treatment, sometimes provoked by coughing, and has missed several follow-up visits. He has taken a statin inconsistently and has not continued antiplatelet and antihypertensive agents reliably. Office blood pressure is markedly elevated. He has resumed limited cigarette smoking and reports previous prostate surgery with persistent urinary or sexual function concerns.

PAST MEDICAL HISTORY: Coronary artery disease status post myocardial infarction and stent placement, hypertension, hyperlipidemia, and prostate surgery.

PHYSICAL EXAMINATION: Blood pressure approximately 179/98.

ASSESSMENT AND PLAN:
1. Coronary artery disease status post stent: Emphasized critical importance of daily clopidogrel to prevent stent thrombosis. Restart clopidogrel and aspirin; reviewed cardiovascular and recurrent infarction risks of nonadherence.
2. Hypertension: Restart therapy cautiously in stages because of reported adverse symptoms. Metoprolol dose ultimately reduced to 100 mg daily after attending review; monitor blood pressure and headaches.
3. Dyslipidemia: Resume simvastatin as tolerated; repeat lipid testing.
4. Headache and medication concerns: Reconcile medicines one at a time to improve adherence rather than stopping the entire regimen.
5. Prostate and erectile symptoms: Return to Urology for consideration of further treatment and device options; relevant prior prescription discussed.
6. Tobacco use: Strongly urged cessation and continued follow-up.
