TITLE: Pulmonary Followup - Chronic Obstructive Lung Disease
DESCRIPTION: Followup for recurrent respiratory exacerbations, inhaler use, environmental triggers, and tobacco use.

HISTORY OF PRESENT ILLNESS: The patient has chronic obstructive airways disease with emergency department visits for respiratory exacerbations in March and June, most recently treated with prednisone and an antibiotic. Nicotine patches are used intermittently, but he declines referral to a cessation clinic. Alcohol intake has decreased from approximately eight to four drinks weekly. He had previously experienced unintended weight loss, but weight is now stable. Earlier imaging performed during that evaluation was reportedly unrevealing apart from degenerative spinal changes. He missed previously scheduled pulmonary function testing and prefers to postpone testing until January.

PAST MEDICAL HISTORY: Chronic obstructive airways disease and prior evaluation for weight loss.

SOCIAL HISTORY: Active smoker; occupational exposure to respiratory irritants. Reports reduced alcohol intake.

PHYSICAL EXAMINATION: Repeat blood pressure approximately 120/90. Other examination findings were not clearly quantified in the conversation.

4. Preventive care. Influenza vaccination and colorectal cancer screening were discussed. The patient declined colonoscopy.

Continue clinical followup and return sooner for worsening respiratory symptoms.
