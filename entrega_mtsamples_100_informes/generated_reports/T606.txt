TITLE: General Medicine Followup - Shoulder Pain and Vascular Evaluation
DESCRIPTION: Chronic shoulder and spine pain, dyslipidemia treatment intolerance, and follow-up imaging of a vascular abnormality.

HISTORY OF PRESENT ILLNESS: The patient presents for medication renewals and ongoing shoulder and back pain. Shoulder movement occasionally produces sharp pain, and prior physical therapy and injections have provided variable relief. He has stopped pravastatin, attributing worsening muscle discomfort to the drug, despite awareness of his cardiovascular risk factors. He continues smoking and reports a history of emphysema. Prior neck imaging showed degenerative changes. A prior examination for possible neurologic symptoms included head imaging, with no confirmed stroke described. The patient is also reluctant to undergo additional vascular imaging because of extensive past testing and claustrophobia, but the clinician has recommended follow-up of an incidental venous abnormality to assess for enlargement or thrombosis.

PAST MEDICAL HISTORY: Hypertension, dyslipidemia, emphysema, chronic musculoskeletal pain and degenerative spinal changes.

ASSESSMENT AND PLAN:
1. Shoulder and back pain: Continue established analgesic and muscle relaxant refills and consider renewed rehabilitation evaluation. Specialist follow-up for possible injection discussed.
2. Dyslipidemia: Reviewed concerns about pravastatin-associated pain and elevated cardiovascular risk off therapy. Patient plans further discussion and reconsideration of treatment.
3. Vascular imaging finding: MR venography recommended and scheduling instructions provided to assess the previously identified abnormality; clinical significance remains uncertain.
4. Smoking and chronic pulmonary disease: Counseled regarding cessation.
5. Preventive care: Tdap booster and routine prescription management reviewed.

Return approximately three months after planned testing and specialist visits.
