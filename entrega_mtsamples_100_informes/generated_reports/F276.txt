TITLE: General Medicine Followup - Cognitive Decline
DESCRIPTION: Assessment of worsening memory and behavioral fluctuations, with family discussion of home-based care.

HISTORY OF PRESENT ILLNESS: An older gentleman returns accompanied by a family member who reports variable memory, occasional paranoia or confusion, and increasing difficulty managing everyday activities. Some days he interacts normally, while on other days he appears disoriented and has poor recall of the preceding evening. He depends on a companion and visiting nursing personnel for medication organization, inhalers, and daily assistance. Traveling to multiple clinic appointments has become increasingly difficult. He reports occasional wheezing but no major new pulmonary complaint. The family asks whether more frequent home services or physician visits would be appropriate. An orientation assessment during the visit suggested difficulty with the current date or year.

PAST MEDICAL HISTORY: Cognitive impairment under investigation and chronic pulmonary disease requiring nebulized medications. Old cerebrovascular changes were noted on prior imaging.

MEDICATIONS: A cognitive medication identified as galantamine and a medication for mood were discussed, along with albuterol and ipratropium nebulizer solutions.

LABORATORY DATA: Recent thyroid function, renal function, liver studies, and blood count were reviewed without significant new abnormalities reported. Vitamin B12 remained pending. Prior imaging showed chronic rather than acute changes.

ASSESSMENT AND PLAN:
1. Progressive cognitive and behavioral symptoms. Arrange evaluation through the walk-in psychiatric or behavioral assessment clinic (PAC) for possible medication or sleep management recommendations. No new psychiatric drug was confirmed at this visit.
2. Increasing care needs. Referral for Home-Based Primary Care and associated nursing or physical therapy support was initiated; availability and family approval remain to be confirmed.
3. Chronic respiratory disease. Renew albuterol and ipratropium nebulizer solutions and other necessary inhaler supplies.
4. Review blood pressure, hydration, pending B12 testing, and any minor electrolyte abnormalities during followup.

Continue care coordination with family and home health providers.
