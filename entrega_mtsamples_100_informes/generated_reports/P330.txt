TITLE: General Medicine Followup - Neuropathic Pain and Anxiety
DESCRIPTION: Ongoing left-sided neuropathic pain with marked anxiety regarding upcoming screening colonoscopy.

HISTORY OF PRESENT ILLNESS: The patient reports persistent severe left lower extremity pain and sensory hypersensitivity, with variable ability to walk and use of a walker. He has been treated with gabapentin and other medications. He describes poor sleep and increased anxiety as his upcoming colonoscopy approaches. His brother, who is undergoing treatment for colon cancer, contributes to his distress. He has resumed drinking alcohol after a period of abstinence, which he relates to worry about the procedure. The patient also notes intermittent congestion relieved by albuterol and concern about his risk of diabetes. Prior neurologic evaluation for painful sensory complaints has been extensive, without a clearly established new explanation.

PAST MEDICAL HISTORY: Hypertension, chronic neuropathic pain, prior episodes of Bell palsy, and other chronic conditions under treatment.

MEDICATIONS: Gabapentin, bedtime medication for sleep and neuropathic symptoms, antihypertensives including amlodipine, hydrochlorothiazide and losartan, and albuterol.

ASSESSMENT AND PLAN:
1. Left-sided neuropathic pain: Reviewed prior work-up and treatment options. Increase amitriptyline from 25 mg to 50 mg nightly; continue other medications as prescribed.
2. Anxiety surrounding colonoscopy: Provided extensive counseling regarding preparation, sedation and screening rationale. Strongly encouraged keeping the scheduled examination given family history.
3. Recent return to alcohol use: Counseled against using alcohol to manage distress.
4. Hypertension: Mildly elevated reading; continue regimen and monitor.
5. Sleep difficulty and mood complaints: Follow clinical response after medication adjustment.

Medication renewals arranged. Follow up in four months, earlier if symptoms worsen.
