Sample Name: Diabetes and Constipation Followup

Medical Specialty:

Consult - History and Phy.

Description: Followup for diabetes and constipation in an elderly patient with prior colon polyps.

(Medical Transcription Sample Report)

HISTORY OF PRESENT ILLNESS: An 84-year-old patient presents for routine followup. He reports that a recently prescribed liquid medication for constipation caused abdominal discomfort without producing satisfactory bowel movements. He has obtained and resumed his previous tablets with benefit and requests the tablets instead. Blood glucose readings are usually in the 90s or low 100s, with a recent morning measurement of 127. He denies worsening chest pain. He occasionally notices small amounts of rectal blood with hard stools attributed to known hemorrhoids. Colonoscopy performed approximately six years earlier showed polyps that were removed. The benefits and burdens of repeat colonoscopy were discussed in view of age and current health status.

MEDICATIONS: Daily aspirin, cholesterol medication, and bowel regimen were reviewed.

REVIEW OF SYSTEMS: No reported unexplained weight loss or black tarry stools. Weight is relatively stable.

ASSESSMENT AND PLAN:
1. Constipation. Discontinue the poorly tolerated liquid agent and resume the previously effective tablets. A prescription for one to two tablets as needed was entered for a 90-day supply with refills.
2. Diabetes. Obtain hemoglobin A1c today. Continue home glucose monitoring.
3. Health maintenance. Schedule ophthalmology and podiatry followup. Previous colon polyps and the possibility of repeat screening were discussed. The patient was advised to report black stools, unexpected weight loss, or other concerning symptoms.
