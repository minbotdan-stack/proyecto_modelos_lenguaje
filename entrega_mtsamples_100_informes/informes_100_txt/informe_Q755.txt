Sample Name: Medical Followup - Status Post Lower Extremity Amputations

Medical Specialty:

Consult - History and Phy.

Description: Medication reconciliation and preventive care after major lower extremity surgery.

(Medical Transcription Sample Report)

HISTORY OF PRESENT ILLNESS: The patient presents for follow-up following substantial lower extremity operations, including a below-knee amputation on one side and removal of toes on the other. He previously had little routine medical care but is now taking numerous medications. He reports adherence to antihypertensive therapy and improvement in blood pressure. He has discontinued alcohol but continues to smoke approximately one pack every one and a half days. He requires refills of several medications and follows with Podiatry for his residual lower extremity problems. Iron replacement was prescribed for marked anemia. He also uses gabapentin, apparently for postoperative or neuropathic symptoms.

PAST MEDICAL HISTORY: Hypertension, anemia, recent lower extremity amputations, and tobacco use.

MEDICATIONS: Antihypertensive therapy including amlodipine and metoprolol; aspirin, gabapentin, iron supplements and vitamins. The regimen was reviewed and simplified where possible.

PHYSICAL EXAMINATION: Blood pressure is satisfactorily controlled.

ASSESSMENT AND PLAN:
1. Status post major amputation and toe surgery: Continue Podiatry and rehabilitation follow-up.
2. Hypertension: Continue treatment. Reviewed metoprolol dosing; a twice-daily schedule was selected because the once-daily preparation was unavailable.
3. Anemia: Continue indicated iron replacement and obtain follow-up blood counts.
4. Medication management: Renew necessary prescriptions, favoring 90-day supplies for adherence.
5. Tobacco use: Encouraged cessation. Alcohol abstinence reinforced.
6. Preventive care: Discussed colonoscopy, which the patient will consider before the next visit. Routine screening laboratory studies requested.

Follow up in two to three months.
