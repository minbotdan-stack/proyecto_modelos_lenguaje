Sample Name: General Medicine Consultation - Erectile Dysfunction

Medical Specialty:

Consult - History and Phy.

Description: Evaluation of erectile dysfunction, preventive screening and psychiatric follow-up after a recent hospitalization.

(Medical Transcription Sample Report)

HISTORY OF PRESENT ILLNESS: A 50-year-old man presents requesting treatment for erectile dysfunction. He has previously used vardenafil and questions the limits on monthly dispensing. He generally receives care through urgent visits rather than an established primary care physician. He reports a recent psychiatric hospitalization after expressing thoughts about overdosing on medications in the setting of stress over a disability-related court hearing. Ongoing psychiatric treatment includes Prozac and mirtazapine, which he finds helpful. Current suicidal ideation was assessed during the visit. He smokes approximately one cigarette per day and expresses intent to stop. He has not previously undergone colonoscopy, although colorectal cancer screening is indicated by age. He reports prior hiatal hernia surgery.

MEDICATIONS: Fluoxetine (Prozac), mirtazapine, and previously prescribed vardenafil.

PHYSICAL EXAMINATION: Blood pressure and general examination were reassuring according to the clinician's assessment.

ASSESSMENT AND PLAN:
1. Erectile dysfunction: Discussed medication safety, blood pressure effects and restricted monthly dispensing. Renew a limited vardenafil supply as planned.
2. Recent suicidal thoughts and psychiatric hospitalization: Encouraged regular mental health follow-up and establishment of continuity in primary care; offered assistance reconnecting with psychiatric services.
3. Tobacco use: Smoking cessation encouraged.
4. Colorectal screening: Refer for colonoscopy and provide scheduling instructions.
5. Preventive health: Obtain updated laboratory testing, including lipid assessment.

Follow up in four months.
