Sample Name: Primary Care Followup - Back and Knee Pain

Medical Specialty:

Consult - History and Phy.

Description: Follow-up for chronic musculoskeletal pain, hypertension, and multiple medical concerns.

(Medical Transcription Sample Report)

HISTORY OF PRESENT ILLNESS: The patient presents with persistent low back and lower extremity pain following prior back surgery and chronic knee pain. Tramadol has provided inadequate relief, and the patient reports taking more than the prescribed amount. He is concerned about pain control and has used ibuprofen previously. He also reports recurrent ear problems requiring ENT care, episodic nocturnal wheezing, numbness affecting the toes of the left foot, and erectile dysfunction despite a trial of vardenafil. He requests review of multiple referrals and medications. He has struggled with weight management and has a history of colon polyps. He reports occasional cigarette and chewing tobacco use.

PAST MEDICAL HISTORY: Chronic back and knee pain, hypertension, obesity, renal function abnormalities, recurrent ear infections, prior colon polyps, and liver-related follow-up.

MEDICATIONS: Tramadol and antihypertensive therapy including a diuretic and enalapril. Vardenafil has been ineffective. Prior ibuprofen use was discussed.

ASSESSMENT AND PLAN:
1. Chronic low back and knee pain: Discussed excessive tramadol use and need to take medication only as directed. An anti-inflammatory drug was considered; however, recent creatinine elevation makes additional NSAID therapy inadvisable pending repeat renal testing. Refer for rehabilitation/pain management and physical therapy.
2. Renal function: Repeat laboratory testing in approximately one week before reconsidering anti-inflammatory treatment.
3. Wheezing: Pulmonary function testing requested.
4. Recurrent ear symptoms: Arrange ENT follow-up.
5. Erectile dysfunction: Trial of sildenafil discussed/prescribed after ineffective vardenafil.
6. Obesity and tobacco use: Encouraged weight-management program and cessation; nicotine replacement discussed.
7. History of colon polyps and liver disease: Continue indicated gastroenterology and liver clinic surveillance.

Follow up in approximately one month.
