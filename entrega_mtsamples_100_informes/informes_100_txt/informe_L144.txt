Sample Name: Chronic Lung Disease Followup

Medical Specialty:

Consult - History and Phy.

Description: Routine follow-up for chronic respiratory symptoms, nocturia, obesity, and stable blood pressure.

(Medical Transcription Sample Report)

HISTORY OF PRESENT ILLNESS: The patient returns with a companion and reports generally stable health. He has chronic pulmonary symptoms treated with maintenance inhalers and a rescue albuterol inhaler; he describes occasional episodes of wheezing or shortness of breath and brief use of an oral medication during exacerbations. He no longer smokes, having quit many years ago. He has access to home oxygen-related equipment and reports limited daily walking. Urinary symptoms include nocturia approximately three times nightly without a clearly new problem starting the stream or emptying the bladder. He also describes longstanding back and joint pain, and recognizes weight reduction and physical activity as important goals. Blood pressure has been satisfactory.

PHYSICAL EXAMINATION: Routine heart, lung, lower-extremity, and blood pressure examination was performed. No alarming acute abnormality was clearly documented in the conversation.

ASSESSMENT AND PLAN: Chronic respiratory disease, currently stable on prescribed inhalers. Reviewed appropriate scheduled maintenance versus as-needed rescue medications and cautioned against unsupervised repeated courses of oral therapies. Continue efforts toward gradual walking, weight management, and avoidance of tobacco. Observe lower urinary tract symptoms and continue routine follow-up and medication refills.
