Sample Name: Diabetes Followup After Hospitalization

Medical Specialty:

Consult - History and Phy.

Description: Routine follow-up after an episode of diarrhea, with low home glucose readings and medication refills.

(Medical Transcription Sample Report)

HISTORY OF PRESENT ILLNESS: The patient reports that diarrhea treated during a recent hospitalization resolved promptly after discharge and has not recurred. He generally feels well and has no active abdominal pain. Although there is a history of diabetes, he is not presently taking glucose-lowering medicine and reports home readings frequently in the 60s to 80s, sometimes accompanied by morning weakness; he uses juice or candy when he feels low. Another measurement after a sweet meal was approximately 116. He sleeps frequently and reports nocturia, associated in part with fluid intake at night. There is mild exertional breathlessness on longer walks without progressive respiratory complaints. The patient has completed advance-directive documents and expresses a preference against intubation; wishes regarding other resuscitation measures remain less clearly specified.

PHYSICAL EXAMINATION: Blood pressure described as good. Cardiac, lung and abdominal examinations conducted without acute abnormalities reported.

ASSESSMENT AND PLAN: Diabetes history with recurrent low readings in the absence of current hypoglycemic medication. Review symptoms and continue glucose monitoring; use oral sugar for symptomatic lows. Repeat glycemic and lipid laboratory studies. No new glucose-lowering therapy planned. Discussed limiting fluids just before bedtime for nocturia. Review advance directives to clarify specific treatment preferences. Renew routine medications and return in six months, sooner for recurrent diarrhea, symptomatic hypoglycemia, or worsening dyspnea.
