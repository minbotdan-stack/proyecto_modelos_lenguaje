Sample Name: General Medicine Followup - Fatigue and Diabetes

Medical Specialty:

Consult - History and Phy.

Description: Followup of daytime fatigue, recurrent low blood glucose, hypertension, sleep apnea, and resumed alcohol consumption.

(Medical Transcription Sample Report)

HISTORY OF PRESENT ILLNESS: The patient reports significant daytime lethargy and frequent sleep despite taking Adderall three times daily. His medication regimen includes psychiatric therapy and intermittent trazodone for sleep. He resumed drinking alcohol approximately three months ago after two and a half years of abstinence. He has difficulty adhering to CPAP therapy and has also gained weight and been less active. He takes metformin 1000 mg twice daily for diabetes but frequently skips meals and reports approximately eight or nine glucose readings in the 40s or 50s, sometimes requiring glucose tablets. Morning readings are otherwise often around the 140s. He also takes lisinopril and metoprolol for hypertension but did not take his scheduled doses on the day of evaluation. He requests further help with alcohol use and psychosocial problems.

PAST MEDICAL HISTORY: Diabetes mellitus, hypertension, obstructive sleep apnea, psychiatric symptoms treated by a psychiatrist, and history of alcohol misuse.

MEDICATIONS: Metformin, lisinopril, metoprolol, Adderall, trazodone as needed, and other psychiatric medications. Ativan was discussed in relation to alcohol. Simvastatin was no longer being taken.

LABORATORY DATA: Recent review indicated normal blood counts, electrolytes, kidney and liver function. LDL was 31 and HDL 62.

ASSESSMENT AND PLAN:
1. Diabetes with recurrent hypoglycemia in the setting of missed meals. Emphasized regular breakfast, lunch, and dinner; do not take the corresponding metformin dose when not eating. Consider decreasing the evening metformin dose from 1000 mg to 500 mg. Seek prompt assessment for repeated symptomatic low readings.
2. Daytime somnolence and obstructive sleep apnea. Fatigue is likely multifactorial. Consistent CPAP use, activity, and weight management were encouraged. Psychiatric medications should be reviewed with his psychiatrist.
3. Return to alcohol use. Strongly advised cessation, psychological or addiction counseling, and avoidance of alcohol together with Ativan or trazodone. Psychology referral was placed.
4. Hypertension. Continue metoprolol and lisinopril; no adjustment was made because prescribed doses had been missed. Recheck blood pressure in two weeks.
5. Dyslipidemia. Lipid panel is favorable; simvastatin was removed from the medication list.
6. Erectile dysfunction. Levitra (vardenafil) was renewed as requested.

Followup for blood pressure in two weeks.
