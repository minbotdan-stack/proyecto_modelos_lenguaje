Sample Name: Chronic Hepatitis C and Cirrhosis Followup

Medical Specialty:

Consult - History and Phy.

Description: Counseling about advanced hepatic fibrosis after unsuccessful hepatitis C treatment, with diabetes and eye care review.

(Medical Transcription Sample Report)

HISTORY OF PRESENT ILLNESS: The patient reports that hepatitis C therapy was unsuccessful and that a liver biopsy and imaging were interpreted as showing advanced cirrhosis. He is anxious about prognosis, liver function, and the possibility of transplantation. He describes episodic severe fatigue and asks whether this relates to hepatic disease, diabetes, or treatment effects. The clinician reviewed the distinction between extensive scarring and current liver function, noting that recent blood tests suggested some preservation of hepatic function and no obvious jaundice. He also has diabetes treated with insulin glargine and metformin. Home glucose values are approximately 115 to 120; the prior A1c was 5.7. The patient reports early glaucoma and dry eyes requiring replacement drops. He has chronic pain and asks about cannabinoid-derived medication, but no such prescription was established.

PAST MEDICAL HISTORY: Chronic hepatitis C with reported advanced cirrhosis after unsuccessful treatment, diabetes mellitus, glaucoma, chronic pain, and prior chest pain with reassuring stress evaluation.

MEDICATIONS: Insulin glargine approximately 25 units daily, metformin 500 mg twice daily, low-dose aspirin, and nitroglycerin as needed. Other previously prescribed medicines were reconciled.

PHYSICAL EXAMINATION: No visible jaundice was described. Lungs were clear on auscultation.

ASSESSMENT AND PLAN:
1. Chronic hepatitis C and cirrhosis. Discussed possible complications, surveillance, and transplantation considerations without declaring a specific prognosis or transplant eligibility. Repeat hepatitis C viral studies and liver function tests in approximately six months, with sooner evaluation for clinical deterioration.
2. Diabetes, well controlled by prior measurements. Continue current insulin and metformin regimen and repeat A1c and renal testing.
3. Glaucoma and dry eyes. Continue regular Ophthalmology surveillance and renew lubricating eye drops as appropriate.
4. Chronic pain and mood concerns. Discussion of alternative symptom management and Mental Health consultation; a cannabinoid medication was not prescribed.

Renew necessary medicines and return in approximately six months.
