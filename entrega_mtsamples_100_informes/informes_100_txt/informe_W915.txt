Sample Name: Medical Followup - Hypertension and Medication Refills

Medical Specialty:

Consult - History and Phy.

Description: Followup for hypertension, chronic joint symptoms, renal specialty care, and supplies for an ostomy.

(Medical Transcription Sample Report)

HISTORY OF PRESENT ILLNESS: The patient returns after wintering in Florida. Home blood pressure readings are generally near 119/79 by his account, but he has been without amlodipine for approximately two months because his prescription had no remaining refills. He reports ongoing specialty followup with infusions and improved, although reportedly still abnormal, blood test values; the precise test results are not available during this discussion. He has chronic ankle and bilateral knee pain attributed to arthritis and remote ankle fractures and occasionally uses a walking stick. He requests renewal of medications and ostomy-related pouches, wafers, and adhesive supplies to cover his periods away from home.

PHYSICAL EXAMINATION: Blood pressure was 158/74 and 154/80 on repeat. The lower extremity swelling appears improved on examination. Heart and lungs were examined. Prior echocardiography was reviewed and showed mild valvular regurgitation.

ASSESSMENT AND PLAN:
1. Hypertension. Elevated office readings in the setting of a prolonged lapse in amlodipine. Restart regular therapy. Arrange adequate refills before his next winter stay in Florida.
2. Ostomy care. Replacement pouches, wafers, and other requested supplies were ordered for delivery to the updated mailing address.
3. Chronic renal and other specialty followup. Continue the previously arranged specialty visits and infusions. Blood tests and a lipid panel were requested today, with results to be reviewed.
4. Degenerative joint discomfort. Chronic symptoms were discussed; no acute change was established.
5. Mild valvular regurgitation on prior echocardiogram. Repeat echocardiography planned at the next followup.

The patient will return in September. He has a scheduled eye appointment and usually receives influenza vaccination while in Florida.
