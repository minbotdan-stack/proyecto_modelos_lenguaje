Sample Name: Followup - Prostatic Symptoms and Medication Refills

Medical Specialty:

Consult - History and Phy.

Description: Routine follow-up for urinary symptoms after cystoscopy, prior cerebrovascular accident, and medication refills.

(Medical Transcription Sample Report)

HISTORY OF PRESENT ILLNESS: The patient returns after urologic assessment and cystoscopy, the results of which were reportedly reassuring. Urinary symptoms and nocturia have improved on finasteride. He has residual left-sided weakness from a prior stroke without a clearly reported new neurologic deficit. He ran out of blood pressure medication but reports good control when taking it. He requests refills, including a previously used nasal preparation. He also mentions a small skin lesion that has been decreasing in size. Prior flexible sigmoidoscopy showed diverticulosis; a complete colonoscopy was discussed, but the patient remains reluctant after discomfort with the earlier procedure.

PAST MEDICAL HISTORY: Previous cerebrovascular accident with left-sided weakness, hypertension, urinary tract or prostate symptoms, and diverticulosis.

MEDICATIONS: Finasteride, lisinopril at one-half tablet daily, amlodipine one tablet daily, and aspirin are specifically reconciled.

PHYSICAL EXAMINATION: Blood pressure described as well controlled. Heart and lung examinations were completed without a reported acute finding.

ASSESSMENT AND PLAN: Continue finasteride and urology follow-up, given symptomatic improvement and reassuring recent cystoscopy. Refill lisinopril and amlodipine and reinforce correct doses. Routine blood tests, including follow-up lipids, ordered. The patient will consider screening colonoscopy but did not commit to the procedure. Observe the improving skin lesion for growth or color change and seek assessment if either develops; dermatology referral was discussed. Routine follow-up after refills and laboratory testing.
